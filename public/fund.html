The Opportunity

We are not another awareness campaign. The YES Movement organises real people into real groups taking real action — on air, water, soil, biodiversity, equity, and carbon. Each group is autonomous, community-led, and connected to a global network that amplifies local impact into something systemic.

Philanthropy has long funded the documentation of crisis. We invite you to fund the architecture of response.

"We think in fractions. Nature thinks in wholes. When we respond with fractional solutions, we get fractional change."

Where Funding Goes

🌱

Group Formation

Seed funding for new YES groups — covering their first summit, tools, and the time it takes to recruit three committed people and log their first action.

🗺

Summit Infrastructure

Grants to host regional summits where groups converge, share breakthroughs, and coordinate across the Nature Six focus areas.

📡

The Telescope

Expanding our intelligence layer — the curated signal feed that tells groups what's working, what science is emerging, and where action is most needed.

⚡

Action Amplification

Turning high-impact local actions into reproducible playbooks and distributing them to every group in the network simultaneously.

Our Commitment to You

We are building in public. Every group, every action logged, every summit held is visible on this platform in real time. You will never wonder where your money went — you will be able to watch it move through the network as groups form, act, and inspire others.

We do not accept funding that comes with conditions on what we say, who we partner with, or which communities we serve. Donors are partners, not controllers.
