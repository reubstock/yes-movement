YES Movement  ·  2025

We think in fractions. Nature thinks in wholes. When we respond with fractional solutions, we get fractional change. The YES Movement is the architecture of a different response.

The diagnosis

We are the first generation to wake up to a world of finite resources. The bills are arriving: climate change first, biodiversity collapse next, then unbreathable air, undrinkable water, mass displacement. Our collective responses have been fractional — project by project, crisis by crisis — while nature operates as a single, interconnected system.

The problem is not a lack of awareness. The problem is a lack of architecture. There is no coordinated, community-rooted, globally connected movement organised around restoration. The YES Movement is being built to be exactly that.

"Nobody gets out of bed to do less bad. But tell us to save the planet, and we're there."

— Douglas Gayeton, Molotov Alva and the Museum of Extraction

The movement

The YES Movement is a coordinated initiative grounded in the Ecological Benefits Framework and the forthcoming book Molotov Alva and the Museum of Extraction by Douglas Gayeton. It operates through four interlocking components, each one reinforcing the others.

01

The Telescope

A twice-weekly broadcast from Substack. The first episode sets the subject and frames the challenge for groups. The second features what groups have done in response — call and answer, week by week.

02

YES Groups

Local groups of up to 16 volunteers who meet to watch, discuss, and act on each broadcast. 12 sessions per season. Their work is uploaded to The Telescope, closing the loop between local action and global signal.

03

The Field Guide

A physical user guide given to every member. Content and worksheets for 12 weeks. Members sketch, sample, measure, and document. The guide makes abstract ecological knowledge tactile and local.

04

Quarterly Summit

Groups converge in person. The first Summit is planned at the headwaters of the Mississippi River around Water. Summits are the movement's exhale — community, synthesis, and renewal.

Who is behind it

The YES Movement is built on fifteen years of work by Douglas Gayeton and The Lexicon, a California-based NGO that spent five years working with Google to develop an accelerator for good ideas — engaging over 1,000 companies and organisations at the intersection of food, agriculture, conservation, and climate change. That work included partnerships with the Gates Foundation, US federal agencies, and hundreds of international partners.

The Museum of Extraction — Gayeton's forthcoming book — is the intellectual and aesthetic foundation for the YES Movement. It names the problem. The YES Movement builds the response.

The budget

The Telescope is the movement's nervous system — the broadcast that sets each week's agenda for every group worldwide. Its production is a professional media operation: three shows per week, short-form social cuts, a quarterly livestreamed Summit, and the infrastructure to distribute it all.

$41K Launch & onboarding
$72K Per month, full production
$31K Per quarterly Summit
$1M Estimated year one

The production team — project lead, senior editor, show producer, social coordinator, assistant editor, tech director, designer — is a lean, professional unit. Costs not yet included: on-air talent, editor-in-chief, talent scout, podcast producer. Year one is the foundation. The model is designed to become self-sustaining through membership and licensing as the network grows.

What we are asking for

We are not running a campaign. We are looking for a small number of founding philanthropic partners who understand that the architecture of a movement — its tools, its broadcast, its Field Guides, its first Summits — must be funded before it can sustain itself. Early partners help determine not just what gets built, but how it gets built.

We believe in complete transparency. Every group, every action, every Summit is visible in real time on this platform. You will not wonder where the funding went. You will watch it become groups, become actions, become a map that grows.
