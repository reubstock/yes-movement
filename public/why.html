Yes.

Not a campaign. Not a coalition. An architecture — the connective tissue of a global restoration movement, built from the ground up, community by community.

Four components. One system.
01

The Telescope

A twice-weekly broadcast setting the agenda for every group worldwide. Call and response — the movement's shared signal.

02

YES Groups

Local groups of up to 16 volunteers meeting weekly to watch, discuss, and act. 12 sessions per season. Their work feeds back into The Telescope.

03

The Field Guide

A physical guide given to every member. 12 weeks of content, challenges, and worksheets. Ecological knowledge made tactile and local.

04

Quarterly Summit

Groups converge in person. Synthesis, community, renewal. The movement's exhale.

Who is behind it

Fifteen years of work. A network already in place.

The YES Movement is built on 15 years of work by Douglas Gayeton and The Lexicon — a California-based NGO that developed the Ecological Benefits Framework in a five-year partnership with Google, building direct relationships with over 1,000 organisations at the intersection of food, agriculture, conservation, and climate change.

The forthcoming book, Molotov Alva and the Museum of Extraction, is the intellectual foundation. It names the problem. The YES Movement builds the response. The team has spent the past year building the platform, curriculum, and broadcast infrastructure needed to launch.
